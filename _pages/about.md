I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Information & Communications Technology, Sungkyunkwan University. My research focuses on **AI-hardware co-design for efficient AI systems**, with an emphasis on **hardware-aware optimization and energy-efficient acceleration for on-device AI**. My work explores optimization across the model, mapping, and dataflow levels, particularly for Compute-In-Memory (CIM) & Processing-In-Memory (PIM) architectures. More recently, I have expanded my research to modern AI workloads, including Large Language Models (LLMs) and 3D/4D Gaussian Splatting (3D/4DGS), with the goal of enabling scalable and energy-efficient AI computing systems.
